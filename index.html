How it works

From brief to live site in three steps

  1. Brief

    You tell me about your business, customers and goals. I suggest the sections, style and colours.

  2. Design & build

    I build the page and share a live preview link, so you can check it on your own phone and send feedback.

  3. Launch

    I put the site online and show you how to change texts, prices and photos yourself.
